# Design Notes from joyousjellyart.com

## Key Design Elements Observed:

### Logo
- Text-based logo: "JOYOUS JELLYART" 
- Appears to use a serif or decorative font
- Brown/dark color
- Dotted border frame around the logo
- Centered in header

### Header
- White/light background
- Centered logo
- Navigation links below logo: "CHRISTMAS COLLECTION", "THE JOYOUS JELLYART STORY", "CONTACT US"
- Search, Account, Cart icons on the right side
- Clean, minimal design

### Typography
- Heading font appears to be a serif/decorative style (similar to Playfair Display or similar)
- Example: "Let your day be filled with what inspires you..."
- "Shop for Christmas" heading uses serif font
- Brown color for headings

### Color Scheme
- Primary: Brown/chocolate tones
- Secondary: Light blue/mint green backgrounds
- Accent: Red for Christmas elements
- Overall warm, earthy palette

### Border Radius
- Appears to use minimal border radius (subtle rounded corners)
- Product cards have slight rounding

### Layout
- Centered content
- Clean white spaces
- Product carousel/slideshow
- Grid layout for products

### Overall Style
- Elegant, artisanal aesthetic
- Warm and inviting
- Focus on product photography
- Minimal, clean design
